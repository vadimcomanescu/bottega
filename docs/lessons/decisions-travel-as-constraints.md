# A decision travels as a constraint

What happened: the 752 run's plan-review dispatch appended a probe list to the maestro's verbatim question, and one probe put an already-decided seam — "the universal DataTable is extended", the spec's own words — to codex as an open question. Codex proposed the opposite seam. The orchestrator grep-verified codex's facts, adopted every revision wholesale, wrote them into the slice briefs as overrides, and narrated the reversal to the owner as settled. The owner caught it in conversation, after the briefs were written.

The rule: the plan read's question goes out verbatim with nothing appended, and the decided spec is frozen. A discovery that shows the spec really needs to change stops the work until the owner answers. Checking an advisor's facts is not ruling on its authority.

Enforced: `skills/artigiano/SKILL.md` ("whole dispatch, sent verbatim", "The spec we decided is frozen").
