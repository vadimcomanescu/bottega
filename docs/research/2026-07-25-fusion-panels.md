# Fusion panels, read against bottega

Snapshot: 2026-07-25. Sources: OpenRouter's Fusion announcement and the ensemble literature around it, each fetched and read this day. This note records what the evidence measured and what bottega concluded, so a later session reads it here instead of researching the concept again. The decision it supports is `docs/adr/0023-panel-is-fusion.md`; the instrument is `skills/panel/SKILL.md`.

## What the sources measured

- [OpenRouter Fusion](https://openrouter.ai/blog/announcements/fusion-beats-frontier/) (vendor benchmark). Same prompt to N models in parallel, a judge emitting structured comparison (consensus, contradictions, partial coverage, unique insights, blind spots), a separate caller writing the final answer. DRACO, 100 deep-research tasks: best solo fable-5 65.3; fable-5 + gpt-5.5 fused 69.0; a three-seat frontier panel 68.3, below the two-seat one; opus-4.8 fused with itself 65.5 against 58.8 solo, a 6.7-point gain and the largest single delta reported. Vendor caveats: judge choice moves absolute scores 10 to 25 points, latency 2 to 3x, not a replacement for coding models.
- [Mixture-of-Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.04692). Same prompt to all proposers, final aggregator synthesizes with an explicit distrust instruction (inputs may be biased or incorrect). Synthesis beats rank-and-select. Proposer ablation 1/3/6 seats: 47.8 / 58.0 / 61.3, so most of the gain arrives by three seats. Proposer skill and aggregator skill are different: one model scored 63.8 as proposer and 52.9 as aggregator.
- [Self-MoA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.00674). All proposals sampled from the single best model beat the mixed panel by up to 6.6 points; in every measured decomposition, proposer quality outweighs proposer diversity. Mixed panels won only among members of near-identical individual strength.
- [Co-failure across 67 frontier models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.27288). Ensemble headroom exists only where models fail on disjoint queries; the same questions have near-zero joint-failure headroom as multiple choice and real headroom as free response. Unweighted voting across 455 triplets had negative mean gain (weaker members outvote the strong one); learned routers captured 9% of the available gain with a confidence interval spanning zero. The gain is reachable only by merging content, not by picking a winner.
- [Voting scaling laws](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.02419). More calls help easy queries and hurt hard ones; panel benefit is non-monotone in seat count.
- [Multi-agent debate measured](https://d2jud02ci9yv69.cloudfront.net/2025-04-28-mad-159/blog/mad/) and [teams hold experts back](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.01011). Every design where members talk to each other lost: debate loses to self-consistency at matched compute, and discussing teams never beat their own best member, with the gap growing as the team grows.
- [LLM-as-judge reliability](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.05685) and [judge geometry](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.03043). Judges carry position bias, verbosity bias, and self-preference (up to 25% inflated win rate for their own text). On subjective rubrics, judges agree with each other more than any of them agrees with humans: consensus among models is coverage evidence, never correctness evidence.
- [Ensembles for code](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.21513). Where an execution oracle exists (tests, compiler), judge-based selection fails to capture the available gain and ensembles often lose to the strongest member; the selection that works is execution-based.

## What bottega concluded

1. The panel pays on open-ended questions where the feared failure is omission: a good answer is a union of considerations, and independent drafts of the same question cover different ones. It does not pay where any checker exists; a test, spike, benchmark, or prototype beats a judge and collapses the headroom. This is the third leg of the panel gate.
2. Identical task text verbatim to every seat, drafts independent, no cross-talk, blinded, a judge that compares and never answers, synthesis by the caller. Every measured win used this shape; every interactive variant lost. This confirms `skills/panel/SKILL.md` as wired and is why differentiated per-seat briefs stay rejected (`docs/adr/0023-panel-is-fusion.md`).
3. Two or three seats of comparable frontier strength. The two-seat panel beat the three-seat one on DRACO; weaker seats subtract.
4. When a second company's seat is unavailable, fuse the best model with itself: two independent runs of one strong model measured the largest single gain in the source. Self-fusion replaces the old answer-solo fallback.
5. Consensus between drafts marks coverage, not correctness. The synthesis checks consensus claims against the repository the same way it checks unique ones.
