# The review closeout runs whole in one seat

The vendored review document is a complete closeout method: review, verify, classify, fix, rerun to a converged head, with its own brakes. Bottega had woven the run's orchestration into it (the maestro verifying each finding, dispatching fixes to fresh builders, an always-on two-engine panel, a rerun engine alternation), which put maestro doctrine inside the review skill and split one meaning across two homes. We decided to adopt the vendored method whole: the run's Review phase dispatches one fresh Opus seat that runs `bottega:autoreview` unmodified over the integrated diff, and the document carries no run.

Consequences worth naming. The maestro rules on the seat's report, not on each routine finding: the scope governor's classes route every promise-changing or scope-breaking finding up, the maestro audits the ruling record when it accepts the converged head, and a misclassification at that boundary is the accepted risk. This supersedes the per-finding ruling of ADR 0035, whose threat-model axis, frozen baseline, and growth numbers stay in the method the seat runs. The panel is the maestro's dispatch-time call, made once, when the diff touches authentication, money, permissions, persisted data, a destructive operation, or anything costly to reverse after merge, and a panel set at dispatch stays the panel on every rerun, replacing the always-panel and the rerun engine alternation. The spec conformance read moved home into the review's Spec lens on the same codex model, so the codex sites of ADR 0032 keep their count. The Standards and Spec lenses join the method as parallel reads whose findings classify like the engine's, and the engine the seat reruns stays stateless while the seat holds the frozen baseline: every engine pass is a cold read of the current diff, which is what makes convergence a fixed point instead of a negotiation.
